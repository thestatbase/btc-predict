WHY THIS EXISTS

Every forecast gets a receipt.

Forecast pages commonly show a number at the moment it is produced. This project stores the number, waits for its stated horizon, and records whether the market closed higher. The resulting history supports a Brier score, a directional hit rate, and a calibration check.

A Brier score measures the squared distance between a stated probability and the eventual binary outcome. Lower is better. A hit rate simply counts whether the model’s side of 50% agreed with the outcome. Neither should be read without the sample size beside it.

THE MODEL, IN PLAIN VIEW

Designed to be inspectable.

01

Volatility state

EWMA and a compact GARCH(1,1) estimate volatility clustering. Higher conditional volatility makes the ensemble more cautious.

02

Filtered trend

A Kalman local-level filter estimates trend velocity; a Hurst approximation changes the balance between continuation and reversal.

03

Horizon-specific ensemble

Readable logistic weights combine standardized 1m and 5m momentum, trend, reversal, and volatility features separately for 1m, 5m, 15m, and 1h.

04

Durable audit

A Cloudflare scheduled job records forecasts and resolves them later. This persistent version does not claim live L2/order-flow coverage.

LIMITATIONS

The honest boundaries.

The scheduled auditor sees one-minute public candles, not a continuously collected full order book. Its statistical record is not a proof of profitable trading and does not include fees, spread crossing, slippage, latency, taxes, or market-impact assumptions. It is educational research, not financial or investment advice.
