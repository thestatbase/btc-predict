METHOD AND LIMITS

Simple enough to inspect.

The live projection uses public one-minute BTC-USD candles. A compact GARCH volatility estimate, Kalman-filtered trend, Hurst-style regime check, and short-window momentum enter explicit horizon-specific logistic weights. The scheduled audit stores one forecast per minute for 1-, 5-, 15-, and 60-minute horizons, then resolves it after the fact.

The persistent auditor does not retain a continuous order-book stream. The model omits trading fees, spread crossing, slippage, latency, taxes, and personal suitability. It is an educational research tool, not investment, financial, or trading advice.
