METHODOLOGY

An ensemble is more honest when its parts remain visible.

At one-minute resolution, Bitcoin is largely a market-microstructure problem: trades consume liquidity, the book replenishes, and quoted prices bounce. As horizons widen, short-lived book signals decay and local trend, volatility state, and slower positioning context deserve relatively more attention. This page follows that division rather than pretending one indicator rules every interval.

1. Volatility is a state, not a constant

The model estimates return variance two ways. RiskMetrics-style EWMA gives recent shocks more weight. A compact GARCH(1,1) likelihood fit then forecasts conditional variance from a long-run level, the most recent squared return, and the last conditional variance. A GJR-style negative-shock term is included because a negative return can plausibly affect the next variance differently from an equal positive return. The model uses this state as a feature and to widen forecast intervals. It does not claim a volatile market is directionally predictable.

2. Trend needs a regime check

A one-dimensional Kalman filter smooths log-price into a local level and velocity without locking the page to a fixed moving-average window. A rolling Hurst estimate, calculated here from a variance-ratio approximation, asks whether recent increments look more persistent or more mean-reverting than a random walk. When the estimate leans toward persistence, trend receives more weight; when it leans toward reversal, the ensemble activates a modest contrarian distance-from-trend feature.

“A probability is useful only alongside the record of what similar probabilities actually did.”

3. The shortest horizon listens to liquidity

Coinbase’s public Level 2 feed supplies near-book depth. The microstructure module measures depth imbalance, quoted spread, and trade-flow imbalance. Trade direction is an approximation: a print at or above the contemporaneous mid is classified as buyer initiated, and one below it as seller initiated. A volume-bucketed VPIN-style imbalance summarizes whether one-sided flow persists. These are deliberately labeled proxies; a browser feed cannot reconstruct a complete institutional order-flow record.

4. Slow context should stay slow

The Crypto Fear & Greed Index, perpetual funding, and mempool fee estimates are fetched only as contextual regime inputs. Their cadences are daily, periodically updated, and block/network-paced—not minute-by-minute alpha. In particular, on-chain congestion is displayed separately and given negligible short-horizon weight. If a public endpoint is unavailable due to a browser or regional restriction, its feature becomes neutral rather than silently substituted.

5. A small logistic ensemble, then an honest scorecard

Each horizon has a readable initial weight vector. It combines standardized modules with a logistic link, not an opaque model. Forecasts are queued and labeled only after their horizon matures. A lightweight online logistic-SGD step can adjust weights during the current browser session; its learning rate is intentionally small and clipped. The audit panel reports the Brier score, directional hit rate, and a probability-bucket reliability table from those resolved forecasts. The confidence interval is a conservative Wilson-style approximation with an effective sample size reduced in high GARCH volatility states.

The design choice is intentional: an empty calibration table is preferable to invented historical skill. Reloading starts a new research session, so the page never presents unverified backtest performance as live evidence.

DATA NOTES

Free, keyless sources—and their limits

Coinbase Exchange provides public BTC-USD ticker, Level 2, and one-minute candle data continuously while the page is open. alternative.me provides the daily Fear & Greed Index. Binance Futures provides its public premium-index/funding field as a slower positioning proxy; if it is unavailable, the feature is neutral. mempool.space provides recommended-fee estimates, refreshed on a minutes-scale cadence. No API key, account, paid feed, backend, persistent tracker, or order-routing capability is used.
